Services & Practices

Comprehensive intellectual property solutions for your business

Trademark Registration

Register and protect your brand name, logo, and slogan under Bangladesh's Trademarks Act 2009. We handle the full process from search to certificate.

  • Trademark Search
  • Application Filing
  • Opposition Response
  • Renewal & Maintenance

Patent Registration

Protect your inventions with comprehensive patent filing services under the Bangladesh Patents and Designs Act 1911.

  • Patentability Search
  • Specification Drafting
  • Patent Filing
  • International PCT Filing

Design Registration

Protect the aesthetic features of your products including shapes, patterns, and colors under Bangladesh's design law framework.

  • Design Search
  • Application Preparation
  • Registration & Certificate
  • Design Enforcement

Copyright Registration

Register copyright for literary, artistic, musical, and software works under the Bangladesh Copyright Act 2000.

  • Copyright Assessment
  • Registration Filing
  • Licensing Agreements
  • Infringement Action

International IP Services

Global IP protection through WIPO's Madrid Protocol for trademarks and PCT system for patents.

  • Madrid Protocol Filing
  • PCT Applications
  • Hague System (Designs)
  • Foreign IP Prosecution

IP Litigation & Enforcement

Aggressive enforcement of your IP rights through Bangladesh courts and alternative dispute resolution mechanisms.

  • Infringement Actions
  • Cease & Desist Letters
  • Court Representation
  • Customs IP Protection
