Our Services

Trademark Registration

Register and protect your brand name, logo, and slogan under Bangladesh's Trademarks Act 2009 from initial search through to certificate issuance.

  • Trademark Search & Analysis
  • Application Filing at DPDT
  • Opposition Response
  • Renewal & Maintenance
  • International Filing (Madrid)

Patent Registration

Comprehensive patent prosecution services under the Bangladesh Patents and Designs Act 1911, including international filing via the PCT system.

  • Patentability Search
  • Specification Drafting
  • Patent Filing at DPDT
  • PCT International Applications
  • Patent Licensing Support

Design Registration

Protect the aesthetic and visual aspects of your products — shapes, patterns, ornamentation — under Bangladesh's design law framework.

  • Design Search
  • Application Preparation
  • DPDT Registration
  • Hague System (International)
  • Design Enforcement

Copyright Registration

Register and protect creative works — literary, artistic, musical, dramatic, and software — under the Bangladesh Copyright Act 2000.

  • Copyright Assessment
  • Registration Filing
  • Licensing & Assignment
  • Infringement Action
  • Digital Copyright Protection

International IP

Protect your IP globally through WIPO's international systems and our network of overseas IP attorneys across 50+ countries.

  • Madrid Protocol (Trademarks)
  • PCT Applications (Patents)
  • Hague System (Designs)
  • Foreign IP Prosecution
  • IP Portfolio Management

IP Litigation & Enforcement

Aggressive protection and enforcement of your IP rights through Bangladesh courts, customs, and alternative dispute resolution mechanisms.

  • Infringement Actions
  • Cease & Desist Letters
  • Court Representation
  • Customs IP Recordal
  • Domain Name Disputes
